Not covered by this calculator
  • Non-residents (in Japan under a year with no home here) and tax treaties.
  • Deductions other than social insurance, spouse and children: life insurance, iDeCo, housing loan, furusato nozei, medical expenses.
  • Tax-free commuting allowance, a second employer, income other than salary, and yearly salary over ¥20 million.
  • Health insurance societies, ages 70 and over, and city-specific resident tax rates.
